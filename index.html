Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Shot on 35 ("the App") is developed by Lucas Henriques ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the App handles your information.

Photos and images

All image processing happens entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any external server. We do not have access to your photos.

Data we collect

The App does not require an account, login, or any personal information to function. We do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Location data
  • Photos or image content
  • Usage analytics or behavioral tracking

Crash reporting

We use Sentry to collect anonymous crash reports. This helps us fix bugs and improve stability. Crash reports may include:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version
  • Technical error information (stack traces)
  • A random anonymous identifier (not linked to your identity)

Crash data does not contain photos, personal information, or anything that identifies you.

Advertising

The App may display ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and use cookies to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device's Google Settings. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

You can remove all ads with a one-time in-app purchase.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not collect or store any payment information.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Third-party services

  • Sentry — crash reporting
  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Google Play — app distribution and payments

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
